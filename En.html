Official Overview

Anatolian Warriors

Anatolian Warriors is an international obstacle course competition inspired by the globally recognized Ninja Warrior format, designed to showcase power, agility, endurance, and mental resilience at the highest level.

The event is organized in Istanbul in collaboration with the Turkish Modern Pentathlon Federation and under the coordination of the T.R. Ministry of Youth and Sports. This structure ensures an organized, fair, and officially recognized competitive environment.

Competition Concept

A premier obstacle course challenge

Anatolian Warriors brings together athletes from different nations on large-scale, professionally constructed obstacle courses. The competition tests:

⚡ Explosive power
🧭 Balance and coordination
🏃 Speed and stamina
🧠 Problem solving under pressure

Each obstacle is engineered to challenge physical and mental limits, creating a dynamic, highly competitive experience comparable to leading international obstacle course events.

Tournament Format

Multi-stage elimination system

Athletes are selected progressively based on performance metrics, completion times, and obstacle success rates. Only top-performing contenders advance to the final stage.

The final round determines the official rankings and champions of the Anatolian Warriors.

Participation & Eligibility

Open to national and international athletes

•Both national and international athletes are eligible to compete
•Contenders are expected to have a background in disciplines such as Parkour, Gymnastics, Calisthenics, Obstacle Course Racing (OCR), or Track & Field
•Athletes must demonstrate a high level of physical preparation and competitive experience
