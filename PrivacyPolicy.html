7. RIGHTS OF THE DATA SUBJECT (KVKK ARTICLE 11)

Within the scope of Article 11 of KVKK, you have the following rights:

1

Right to Be Informed

To learn whether your personal data is being processed

2

Right of Access

To request information about your processed data

3

Right to Learn the Purpose

To learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in accordance with that purpose

4

Right to Know Third Parties

To know the third parties to whom your data has been transferred

5

Right to Rectification

To request correction of incomplete or inaccurately processed data

6

Right to Deletion and Destruction

To request deletion or destruction of data within the scope of Article 7 of KVKK

7

Right to Notification

To request that correction/deletion actions be notified to third parties

8

Right to Object

To object to a result against you arising from analysis by automated systems

9

Right to Compensation

To request compensation for damages suffered due to unlawful data processing
